# Visual world: The Workshop Desk

Dolphin's interface lives in the technical learner's private study desk: warm paper, wool felt, brass and steel, precise ink. The student chose this direction from the concept round. It deliberately rejects the two looks the category ships by default — the SaaS AI dashboard and the hacker terminal — both named "wrong" in the ask round, and clutter generally.

The committed world:

- **Color**: restrained — warm paper neutrals with one brass accent. Light surface (a desk under a warm evening lamp), not dark.
- **Type**: a workhorse text face for long-form lessons plus a precise mono for code and labels. No display-face theatrics.
- **The Library (first surface)**: a shelf of bound course notebooks, each with its topic and a progress ribbon, plus a blank "New course" notebook and a small status light for the local agent.
- **Reading**: a well-set page — generous measure, the Tutor's notes in the margin, code in mono.
- **Generation**: honest, legible progress (lessons appearing one after another), never a spinner.

Risk to guard: "tactile" must stay precise and legible, never decorative.

Challengers considered and set aside: Paper Folds (Miura), Stage Light (Cyclorama), Silkscreen Poster (WPA). Three further dealt worlds were rejected: two are the terminal look already ruled out, and one is too busy and console-like.
